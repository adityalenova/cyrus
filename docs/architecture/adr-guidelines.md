# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) captures an important architectural decision made along with its context and consequences.

## Structure
1. **Title**: Numbered sequentially (e.g., `ADR-001: Next.js App Router`).
2. **Status**: Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, Superseded.
3. **Context**: What problem are we solving? What constraints exist?
4. **Decision**: What did we choose to do?
5. **Consequences**: Positive, neutral, and negative outcomes of this decision.
