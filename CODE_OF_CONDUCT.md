# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone taking part here is welcome, whatever their experience level,
background or identity. This project is small and run by one maintainer, which
is exactly why the tone has to stay deliberate.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. A question about someone's code is not an attack on it.
- Critique the change, never the person.
- Give context when you ask for something, and take "not now" as an answer.
- Keep the space for people who are here to work on the project.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal or political attacks, insults, unwelcome attention, and
any publication of private information without consent. Also unacceptable:
doing any of the above while pretending to give feedback.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainer through GitHub. Complaints are read, answered
and acted on proportionally, from a warning up to a temporary or permanent ban
from the repository. If a report concerns the maintainer, say so plainly and it
will be handled accordingly.
